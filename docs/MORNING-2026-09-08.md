## 4. Build 3 — timing marks

The rehearsal brief now proposes two things per line: the **transition phrase** that hands off
into the next slide, painted yellow, and the **cue word** to change the slide on, painted
orange. Both must really appear in the line or they're thrown away.

To set them yourself, select any words in the line — a small toolbar appears with *transition
phrase*, *cue word*, *clear*. A plain click still opens the editor; a drag-select never does.

They paint in the teleprompter pop-out and in the film window's prompter panel. A shortened or
edited line keeps only the marks that still fit it.

Also: after two rounds, the plain textarea on the film page is gone. In its place is the
review's own Suggested card, so the line gets **Say it**, the ✂ shorten passes, the version
chips with ↶, and the mark toolbar, without leaving the film surface.

## 5. The outro CTA

Under surviveaccounting.com there's now a big red pill — **Start cramming free** — with
*Videos · Practice Exams · Quizzes* beneath it. It breathes a soft red glow at rest and lifts
with a sheen on hover.

The red is `#E63B2D`. The house had no usable red left: the two that existed were picked for
cream paper and go maroon on black. This one stays red at video bitrates and leans toward
orange so it reads as a neighbour of the gold rather than fighting it.

**The spotlight:** ctrl+click the pill on /film. The wordmark's bolt throws three jagged trunks
with forks into the top edge of the button, flickering out over about seven tenths of a second.
The pill overshoots, flashes white-hot, settles red-hot, and from then on a bright streak sweeps
through it every nine tenths of a second with sparks flickering off the rim. Ctrl+shift is the
super: five trunks, four forks each, a gold halo instead of blue. Backtick or the next slide
clears it, like every other spotlight.

It's seeded, so two takes render identical pixels.
