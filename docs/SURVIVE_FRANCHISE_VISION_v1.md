## THE THESIS

Survive is not building a video library. It is building four engines
that, together, form a franchisable system for dominating weed-out
courses:

1. **CONTENT ENGINE** — the Survive Method: CEQs ordered to teach,
   reusable exhibits, Blast Off/Jam/Vibe, shorts, the phrase bank, the
   production conveyor (talk → transcribe → AI starter → human final →
   film → publish).
2. **GROWTH ENGINE** — outreach + YouTube + shorts distribution at
   scale. King's lane (see below).
3. **PLATFORM ENGINE** — the site/player/studio: the machinery that
   makes one tutor's teaching feel like a polished product and makes
   the next tutor's onboarding nearly turnkey.
4. **DISTRIBUTION NETWORK** — Greek chapters as storefronts. "When our
   flyer sits up at a Greek house and we get into the culture of that
   house, they're going to want more. McDonald's has stores; we have
   chapters."

The moat compounds: methodology + internal philosophies + trade secrets
+ marketing ability + distribution. Content alone is copyable; the
SYSTEM is not.

## THE ROLES (current)

- **Lee** — product development, pedagogy, teaching style, brand, UI/UX,
  marketing copy. The face of the brand and keeper of the method. "I
  provide the map."
- **King** — the growth engine. Campus outreach (Instagram, email, per
  campus), YouTube channel growth management, shorts distribution.
  "You drive the car — and then turn it into a Waymo." 80%+ of King's
  time on growth; he's also invited into the idea culture: dictate
  growth-engine ideas into the same tools Lee uses, bring back ideas
  that are HIS.
- Team principle: human judgment stays visible. AI multiplies; it never
  replaces the teacher of record.

## THE PHASES

**NOW (Fall 2026)** — prove it at Ole Miss. Push Intro 1 to as many
campuses as the quality bar allows. Learn everything: conversion,
practice-attempt rate, what students ask, what spreads in chapters.
Finish one exceptional canonical Exam 1 before scaling tailoring.

**NEXT (2027)** — same-student expansion (Intro 2/managerial — Lee
likely teaches it himself to keep the creative engine warm), begin
recruiting the FIRST partner tutor for Finance. Build intermediate
eventually because the A-students are already asking ("people already
ask right now") — intermediate is for the ones who want the A; Intro 1
should be so good most students don't need Lee twice.

**FRANCHISE PROOF (target 2028, hard floor 2030)** — at least one
franchisee live, most likely **Survive Finance**. Lee involved in
development ("pop in — hey, let's talk through this for a sec"), stays
the face of the network. Prove a seven-figure operator business so the
next tutors WANT the system: "there's going to be demand from awesome
tutors to try out for this."

**THE SCHOOL (beyond)** — Survive as a multi-course school of weed-out
courses only: intro accounting, intermediate, finance, chemistry, orgo,
biology. New courses shipping every summer. Domain note: survive
prep.com flagged as the likely umbrella grab if/when the network needs
one (unverified availability — check before acting).

**THE EXIT SHAPE (Lee's stated dream)** — build to franchisable proof,
then sell or step back: recruit and train the best tutors, teach the
philosophy in summer workshops, remain the face lightly, "collect a
check, be proud of what I built, work on other projects, travel, enjoy
life." The business should be built so this is possible — documented
method, transferable system, a team that runs the engines.

## OPERATING COMMITMENTS THAT PROTECT THE VISION

- Everything important gets DOCUMENTED and INDEXED — method, manual,
  vision, growth playbooks — viewable inside the product for the team
  (a future internal Library/Vision page; categories stay few: teaching
  philosophy · product · franchise · marketing/growth · platform).
- Raw dictation transcripts are FIRST-CLASS ARTIFACTS, stored verbatim
  forever. Summaries are derived and never replace them. (Lesson
  learned: a summarizing chat tool flattened months of voice. Never
  again.)
- Every idea becomes a hypothesis; the product is a research project.
  Data replaces guesses after beta, not before.
- The quality bar gates expansion. One bad chapter experience poisons a
  campus's Greek network; content completeness gates new campuses.
- Proudly exclude. The brand does the hardest courses and says so.

## SUCCESS, PLAINLY

Thousands of students a semester who stopped being scared of the course
that was built to scare them. A method good enough that other tutors
audition for it. A company Lee could hand off without it losing its
soul — because the soul is written down.
