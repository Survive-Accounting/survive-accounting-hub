## What Survive is

Survive Accounting (surviveaccounting.com). Lee Ingram — Ole Miss BAccy/MAccy, tutor since 2015,
1,000+ students — makes vertical short videos ("Like Reels for exam prep.") for intro
financial accounting. Exam 1 is free. Topics for Exam 1, in order: Easy Points (Start Here) ·
Recording Journal Entries · Adjusting Entries · Financial Statements · Closing Entries ·
Principles & Vocab. Each topic is a **cram path** of short videos, each video one **split** of a
set, three minutes or less, then practice questions (CEQs: "common exam questions") at the end
of the topic. Deeper videos are **offshoots** ("Take it to an A"), made later on request.

Brand: black ground, cream text, the red/blue **bolt** (the "i" in the wordmark). Campus
colours come in when a school is known. Voice: plain, direct, no emoji, no cringe, "Use your
words". Slogans used verbatim on slides: "This is a cram video—not a lecture." and the
outro line. Copy never says "run", "blast", or "pledge" to students.

## A video, frame by frame

A split = bookends + cards + callouts, in a 9:16 frame with safe zones (status bar top, the
like/share rail on the right, caption/title/sound at the bottom, a captions box at the
bottom-left). Kinds that exist today:

- **open** (cold open) · **intro** ("5 Types of Accounts" + wordmark, camera hero) · **bio**
  · **outro** (sign-off).
- **ceq** — a card: stem + choices; the correct one lights on reveal.
- **phrase** "Memorize this" · **cheat** "Cheat code" (title + bullets) · **tip** "Go deeper" ·
  **tricky** "Tricky question" · **found** "Found on your exam" · **slogan** · **blank** ·
  **exhibit** · **bolt** (a bolt animation detour) · **ad** · **cluster** (a map).
- Reveals are stepped by the spacebar while filming; each kind decides what appears per step.
- Camera per kind: corner circle, home circle, hero rectangle (intro), or off (slogan, outro).
