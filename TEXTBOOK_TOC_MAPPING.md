# Textbook TOC → Survive-Topic Mapping (PROPOSED — not applied)

_2026-08-25 · branch `overnight/course-intel-harvest`. These are PROPOSED mappings for human review.
Nothing here is written to any student-facing exam/topic/chapter map. The point: normalize the
different chapter numberings of the dominant Intro-Financial textbooks onto **one canonical Survive
topic spine**, so "Exam 1 = Ch 1–4" resolves to the same Survive topics regardless of textbook._

The top ~5 editions below cover the large majority of the campuses with textbook evidence (per
`COURSE_INTEL_TEXTBOOK_TOC_WORKLIST.csv`). Chapter titles are the editions' standard TOCs.

---

## Canonical Survive topic spine (Intro Financial)

`S1` Financial statements & the accounting equation · `S2` Transaction analysis / recording (journal
entries, T-accounts) · `S3` Adjusting entries & accrual basis · `S4` Completing the cycle / closing ·
`S5` Merchandising operations · `S6` Inventory & COGS · `S7` Internal control & cash · `S8` Receivables ·
`S9` Long-lived assets (PP&E, intangibles) · `S10` Liabilities (current + bonds) · `S11` Stockholders'
equity · `S12` Statement of cash flows · `S13` Financial-statement analysis.

## The payoff: a normalized Exam-1 topic set

Across **all four dominant textbooks**, "Exam 1 = Chapters 1–4" resolves to the **same Survive topics**:

> **Exam 1 (normalized) = S1 Financial statements & the equation · S2 Transaction analysis / journal
> entries · S3 Adjusting entries & accrual basis · S4 Completing the cycle.**

That is the film target, and it is textbook-independent. Exam 2 normalizes to **S5–S8**
(merchandising, inventory, internal control & cash, receivables); Exam 3 to **S9–S11** (long-lived
assets, liabilities, equity); Final adds **S12–S13** (cash flows, analysis).

## Review / caveats (why this is PROPOSED, not applied)
- OpenStax's extra front context chapter shifts its numbering by ~1 — confirm per campus using its
  actual code before trusting a raw "Ch 1–4".
- A handful of campuses use custom/older editions or "Financial & Managerial" combined books where the
  managerial half renumbers — exclude those from the Exam-1 normalization.
- Survive's internal unit IDs should be attached by a human in the admin review step (the `S#` spine
  here maps cleanly to Survive's financial units; the managerial units U14–U24 are out of scope for
  Intro-1 Exam 1–3).
- Recommend seeding these as `textbook_chapter_topic_mapping` rows with `state='proposed'` for admin
  approval — **not** writing them into any live student map.
