### P1 — fix before merge
1. **Admin server functions have no server-side authorization.** `growth-admin.functions.ts` (9
   `createServerFn`) and `referral-admin.functions.ts` (15) use the **service-role key** and are
   gated **only** by the client-side `<AdminGate>` (a localStorage passcode shipped in the public
   bundle — its own source calls it "a deterrent, not real security"). Neither verifies a Supabase
   admin session the way `decideChapterClaim` does (`adminEmailFromToken`). Referral exposes
   **commission (money)** data and contact phone numbers; Growth exposes contacts + revenue rollups.
   RLS is enabled on the new tables but with **0 policies**, which does nothing against a
   service-role caller. → Add the token/`adminEmailFromToken` check to every mutating (and
   sensitive read) function before these branches deploy. *(Not auto-fixed: 24 functions + the
   AdminGate→session decision is a design change, not a safe mechanical fix.)*
2. **Two branches ship `OVERNIGHT_REPORT.md`** with different content → merge conflict on the second.
   Rename before merging (e.g. `OVERNIGHT_REPORT_GROWTH.md` / `_REFERRAL.md`).
3. **Two DDL migrations are unapplied** and their features do not work until Lee pastes them:
   `growth_contacts/roles/outreach_events` (Growth degrades gracefully — verified `42P01` handling)
   and the whole `referral_*` set (Referral is **entirely inert** — no fallback). See
   NEW_ENV_REQUIREMENTS.md for the apply list.
